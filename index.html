Budapest, Saturday evening

Meet at Florin Apart Hotel, Károly krt. 9. Sunset is at 5:45pm, so we need to be up the hill by 5:15.

  1. 3:30

    Bors Gastro Bar

    Kazinczy u. 10, five minutes from the hotel. Soups and baguettes, about €8 each.

    Open in Google Maps
  2. 4:45

    Bus 16 from Deák Ferenc tér

    Two minutes from the hotel. Ride about 15 minutes up Castle Hill and get off at Szentháromság tér.

    Open in Google Maps
  3. 5:15

    Fisherman’s Bastion

    Find a spot on the lower terraces (free) before sunset at 5:45. Stay until Parliament lights up across the river. Matthias Church is right beside it.

    Open in Google Maps
  4. 6:30

    Buda Castle

    Ten minutes’ walk south along the hill. The courtyards and terraces are open and lit at night, and free to wander.

    Open in Google Maps
  5. 7:15

    Chain Bridge

    Walk down beside the funicular and cross on foot. Stop in the middle for the lit-up view both ways.

    Open in Google Maps
  6. 7:45

    Karaván, then Szimpla Kert

    Straight back along Károly körút to the Jewish Quarter. Street food at Karaván (Kazinczy u. 18), then the ruin bars next door. Both are a few minutes from the hotel.

    Open in Google Maps

Bring a jacket, it gets cold on the hill after sunset.

Everything after the bus is walking and free. Bus 16 needs a normal ticket or the 24-hour travelcard.
